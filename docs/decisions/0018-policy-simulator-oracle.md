# ADR-0018: The policy simulator is the mapstate oracle

Date: 2026-10-03. Status: accepted. Resolves #103 (spec 06 §12 item 12).

## Decision

Adopt spec 06's recommendation: an independent brute-force evaluator is the
oracle for policy map state, and every mapstate or frontend optimization is
gated on agreement with it. The alternative, trusting a port of the reference
algorithm, has no independent check and is rejected.

Following the precedent of ADRs 0011–0013, acceptance is the recorded choice
and its normative specification, not completion of the policy compiler. This
record does not claim Kubernetes policy enforcement or BPF execution.

## What exists (verified at the revision recorded on #103)

- `oracle::evaluate` is the oracle: a direct scan where the highest precedence
  rule wins and deny beats allow at equal tier/priority.
  `mapstate::MapState` is an optimized interval index built separately. It uses
  only the oracle's shared types and `validate_subject`, never `oracle::evaluate`
  or its ranking helpers.
- `simulator` resolves selected subjects, peers and named ports. Its direct path
  calls the oracle; its compiled path expands into `MapState`.
- `kernel_map` holds a second, lower-level oracle: a full scan over canonical
  `PolicyKey`/`PolicyEntry` records, compared against the optimized `Index`.
- Gates run as mandatory `cargo test -p flowsdn-policy` tests:
  - `tests/mapstate.rs`: the exhaustive comparison over all 65,536 ports.
  - `tests/simulator.rs`: all 27 harvested `FuzzDistillPolicy` seeds, with
    reversed order and mutations, plus 128 generated byte streams, through both
    frontend paths.
  - `tests/kernel_map.rs`: the deterministic scan/index soak (default 256 maps;
    `FLOWSDN_POLICY_SOAK_CASES` raises it).

## Rule

A change to `mapstate`, the `simulator` frontend, the `kernel_map` index or its
pruning merges only if these gates pass. A new optimization adds its own
differential case against the oracle rather than a test of its expected output
alone.

## Remaining obligation (milestone 2, #292)

The complete Rule-to-kernel compiler (spec 06 §§5.4–5.8) does not exist yet.
Before its optimizations can claim end-to-end agreement, it must be connected to
both the frontend simulator and the kernel-entry oracle. Missing pieces include:

- same-identity precedence normalization, cross-identity covering insertion and
  Pass reranking;
- incremental origin/cookie handling, and authentication inheritance beyond the
  partial helper;
- full selector import.

Coverage-guided fuzzing, as distinct from the bounded deterministic generators
above, also remains to be added. These obligations are mandatory under spec 06
and part of #292's acceptance. They are implementation work under a settled
decision, not an open question.
